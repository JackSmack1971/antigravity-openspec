# /opsx:explore — Non-Structured Investigation

## Purpose
Acts as a thinking partner enabling non-structured investigation. Used when an idea is not yet fully formed or when brainstorming is required before committing to the OpenSpec artifact architecture.

## Workflow
1. **Investigation:** Agent engages in unstructured dialogue, research, or prototyping with the user.
2. **Transition:** Once the idea reaches maturity and artifacts must be created, the agent smoothly transitions to `/opsx:propose` (for automatic generation) or `/opsx:new` (for manual scaffolding).
