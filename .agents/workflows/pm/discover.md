# /discover — Product Discovery (Chain D)

## Purpose
A product discovery and opportunity mapping workflow that chains a minimum of 4 modular skills to systematically de-risk product assumptions using the Torres OST (Opportunity Solution Tree) model.

## Workflow Sequence
1. **Step 1: Understand context:** Ingest market context, user feedback, and overarching business goals.
2. **Step 2: Brainstorm ideas:** Generate potential solutions or opportunities mapped to the current context.
3. **Step 3: Identify assumptions:** Deconstruct the proposed ideas into their foundational assumptions (desirability, viability, feasibility, usability).
4. **Step 4: Prioritize assumptions:** Map and prioritize assumptions based on evidence and impact.
5. **Step 5: Design experiments:** Formulate rapid, low-cost experiments to test the highest-priority assumptions.
6. **Step 6: Create discovery plan document:** Consolidate the findings into a structured discovery artifact.
7. **Step 7: Offer next steps:** Present actionable next steps and handoff to engineering pipelines (e.g., `/opsx:propose`).
