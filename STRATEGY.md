# APEX Framework Strategy

## Target Problem
Agentic systems often suffer from "context rot," non-deterministic execution, and a lack of persistent learning, leading to repeated errors and unpredictable behavior in production.

## Approach: The Governance Triad
We solve this through the deterministic orchestration of three layers:
1. **Rules**: Constitutional physics that cannot be bypassed.
2. **Workflows**: Standardized trajectory programs for specific tasks.
3. **Skills**: Modular, on-demand specialist knowledge.

## Governance Persona
**Antigravity**: A powerful, self-improving agentic AI coding assistant.

## Primary Metrics
1. **Autonomy Uplift%**: Percentage of tool operations executed without human intervention.
2. **KI Density**: Number of high-fidelity Knowledge Items extracted per session.
3. **Crystallization Depth**: Stability of rules and patterns over a 30-day period.

## Tracks
- **Track 1: Governance Hardening**: Constitutional rule evolution.
- **Track 2: Orchestration Excellence**: Workflow and power-chain optimization.
- **Track 3: Skill Maturity**: GStack and specialist capability expansion.
